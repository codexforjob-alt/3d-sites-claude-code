    How we actually work

    We shoot motion control because take two should be identical to take one. When a client changes their mind at the grade, we can go back and match the move exactly — no reshoot, no argument.

    We grade in-house. Handing the footage to a third party is where most films lose their nerve; the contrast gets safe, the blacks lift, and the thing you shot at 2 a.m. turns into stock.

    Six people, one room. You talk to the director, the DP and the colourist. There is no account manager between you and the person holding the camera.
